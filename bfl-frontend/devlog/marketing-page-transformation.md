## Why This Transformation Is Needed
The current marketing page is structured like a product/service offering rather than telling the story of a non-profit collaboration. It uses commercial language like "our platform," "request a demo," and "pricing" which doesn't align with the non-profit nature of the initiative. We need to shift the focus to the partnership story, the mission, and the real impact being made.

## Component-by-Component Transformation Plan

### 1. Hero Section
- **Current**: Product-focused with generic "AI-Powered Insights" messaging
- **Transform to**: Partnership story-focused with specific mission statement
- **Changes needed**:
  - Update headline to focus on the collaboration
  - Replace "Request Demo" CTA with "Join Our Mission" or "Support Our Work"
  - Add brief explanation of who Brazil Flying Labs and Boone Voyage are
  - Include a compelling statistic about rainforest loss or recovery efforts

### 2. Features Section
- **Current**: Generic product features
- **Transform to**: Specific technologies and approaches being used in the field
- **Changes needed**:

### 3. Benefits Section
- **Current**: Generic benefits for "conservation teams"
- **Transform to**: Real impact stories and outcomes

### 4. How It Works Section
